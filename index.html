Common Questions

Straight answers to the questions Covina homeowners ask us most. Something else on your mind? Just call.

My pipes are leaking. What should I do first?

Shut off the nearest water valve or the main shut-off, move anything that can be water damaged out of the way, and call us. The only way to know for sure how bad a leak is and where it comes from is to have a plumber look at it, because many things can cause water damage that are not visible.

Can cast iron plumbing be repaired?

Yes, in many cases cast iron lines can be repaired without a full replacement. We inspect the line with a camera first so you see exactly what is wrong, then recommend repair or replacement based on the condition of the pipe.

What does drain cleaning involve?

We clear the blockage with the right tool for the job, from a drain snake for a simple clog to hydro jetting for heavy grease and root buildup. A camera inspection can confirm the line is fully clear when it is done.

What is hydro jetting and camera inspection?

Hydro jetting blasts the inside of the pipe with high-pressure water to remove grease, scale, and roots. A camera inspection sends a small camera down the line so you can see the exact problem before we fix it. It is our newest service for Covina homes.

How much does a plumbing repair cost?

Every job is different, so we give an honest upfront price before any work starts. Our whole business is built on affordable pricing, and small or large plumbing jobs are handled on the same service call so you are not paying for a second visit.

Do you handle both small and large plumbing jobs?

Yes. We are your Covina plumber for small fixes and large projects alike, and we handle jobs of any size on the same service call whenever possible.
